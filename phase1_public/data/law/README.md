# Legal source manifest

The local corpus is organised by normative authority and applicability:

1. Level 1: primary laws.
2. Level 2: administrative regulations.
3. Level 3: departmental regulations and supplementary technical material.
4. Level 4: local regulations and standards, enabled only when project location,
   project type, and scope are confirmed.

GB/T 50500 is retained as S2 supplementary/practice material pending source
verification. It cannot independently support a legal conclusion.

The national laws-and-regulations database is the preferred external fallback
or verification source. CECN is a candidate industry-information source and
cannot be used as an independent legal basis. External results require manual
confirmation and provenance.
