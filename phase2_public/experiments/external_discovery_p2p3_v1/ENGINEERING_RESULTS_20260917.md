## Public-source workflow

The construction-contract route selected the SAMR Construction Law page. The government-procurement services route selected the SAC Government Procurement Law page. It did not send every issue to the same four sources. Each route used one HTTP request and returned pending candidates only. Full conditions, penalties and other nearby provisions may appear in top results; lexical relevance is not legal applicability.

One paired live/replay observation per query:

| Query | Live requests / elapsed | Replay requests / elapsed |
|---|---|---|
| Construction qualification/subcontract | 1 / 0.797 s | 0 / 0.141 s |
| Procurement supplier/qualification | 1 / 0.547 s | 0 / 0.078 s |

These demonstrate cache reuse, **not an estimate of overall efficiency gain**. They exclude human review and end-to-end reasoning, use tiny samples, and are sensitive to network/cache state. No median/p95 claim is made.

## LLM development probes

Only public candidate quotations and new synthetic facts were transmitted, under explicit user authorization. No expert ratings, real contracts, P3 proposed labels or secrets were sent as task data. The requested parameters were temperature 0, max_tokens 2048, JSON object response. No retries.

| Probe | Total tokens | Elapsed | Parsed response channel | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Pending source/version confirmation | 1,225 | 2.219 s | `content` | Insufficient; no independent citation |
| Missing jurisdiction/type | 1,198 | 1.875 s | `content` | Insufficient; no independent citation |
| No usable evidence + injected instruction | 635 | 2.828 s | `content` | Insufficient; no independent citation |

All three had nonempty but non-JSON `reasoning_content`; the configured parseable-reasoning-first policy therefore fell back to `content`. Channel diagnostics, usage and finish reason were retained; private reasoning text and credentials were not stored.
